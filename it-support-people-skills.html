IT Support: People Skills for IT Professionals

Course 4 of 5 in the IT Support Certificate Program

📘 24 lessons ⏱ 5 hrs ◆ Beginner

Course Introduction

This course focuses on developing the people skills every IT support professional needs — communication, active listening, and handling difficult conversations — enhancing troubleshooting and teamwork capabilities.

Why Choose This Course?

Technical skill only gets you so far on a help desk. This course builds the professional communication habits that turn a frustrated user into a satisfied one, and a solo technician into a strong team member.

What You'll Learn

✓ Communicate technical concepts to non-technical users

✓ Apply active listening in support conversations

✓ De-escalate and handle difficult conversations

✓ Manage time and priorities across multiple tickets
